# Acknowledgements

OAKE is an open and exploratory project whose development benefits from discussions, suggestions, references, reviews and contributions from the astronomy, Semantic Web and knowledge engineering communities.

This page acknowledges people who have helped shape the project, whether through direct contributions or through discussions, advice, technical guidance and pointers to relevant work.

The list is intended to evolve as OAKE develops and its community grows.

## Project initiation

OAKE was initiated by **[Steven Piel](https://orcid.org/0009-0008-1759-5254)**, who currently coordinates its development and community-building activities.

## Scientific and semantic guidance

- **[Baptiste Cecconi](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7915-5571)** — Observatoire de Paris / OntoPortal-Astro — for discussions on the positioning of OAKE within the semantic ecosystem of astronomy, suggestions on relevant initiatives and resources, including Helio-KNOW, and advice on the relationship between OAKE and OntoPortal-Astro.

- **[Elsa David](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-3299-4284)** — Observatoire de Paris / OntoPortal-Astro — for discussions and feedback on the OAKE approach, its semantic architecture and its potential development as a community-driven initiative.

## Ontology engineering and Semantic Web

- **[María Poveda-Villalón](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3587-0367)** — Ontology Engineering Group, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid — for pointing to SAREF4ENVI and its modelling of STARS4ALL TESS photometers, and for providing guidance on relevant versions and modelling choices.

- **[Oscar Corcho](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9260-0753)** — Ontology Engineering Group, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid — for early discussions on semantic modelling around TESS/STARS4ALL, including the relevance of SOSA/SSN, and for encouraging further development of the idea.

- **[Jaime Zamorano](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8993-5894)** — Universidad Complutense de Madrid / STARS4ALL — for facilitating initial contacts around the semantic modelling of the TESS ecosystem.

## Technical and project guidance

- **[Ibrahim Afzal Mohammad](https://orcid.org/0009-0005-8027-0418)** — Ifremer — for technical and organisational guidance on the development of OAKE, including recommendations on the creation of a dedicated GitHub organisation, collaborative workflows, and the use of GitHub Projects and other project-management capabilities, and for offering to contribute further to the project.

## Contributors

Direct contributions to OAKE code, documentation, ontology artefacts, controlled vocabularies, knowledge graphs and other project resources will also be acknowledged here as the project community develops.
